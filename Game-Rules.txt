THE TAIPING REBELLION — CLASSROOM GAME RULES
Two teams · 12 years · 2 actions per team per year
Play: https://littlecrawler.github.io/hy2206-taiping-game/

1. TEAMS AND STARTING POSITION
Taiping is red; Qing is blue; neutral cities are grey.
Both teams start with 3 cities.
Taiping: 10 total strength (6 + 4), with 2 reinforcement tokens.
Qing: 12 total strength (5 + 5 + 2), with 3 reinforcement tokens.
These are classroom balance settings, not historical troop numbers.

2. TURN ORDER
The game runs from 1853 to 1864, inclusive: 12 annual rounds.
Each year: Taiping action 1 → Taiping action 2 → Qing action 1 → Qing action 2 → next year.
The two actions belong to the TEAM. Use one army twice or split them between armies.
Use both actions before clicking Next team. The opponent cannot act after only your first action.
The coloured banner shows whose turn it is and how many actions remain.
An immediate victory ends the game even if actions remain.

3. CHOOSE ONE OF THREE ACTIONS
Each move, attack or reinforcement costs 1 action.
Selecting armies or destinations, viewing history cards and changing the map's route filter are free.
Route filters only highlight routes; they do not restrict where you can play.

4. MOVE AND CAPTURE
Select your army, then one directly connected city.
If there is no enemy army there, move along that single connection.
An enemy or neutral city with no defending army is captured automatically. No separate Claim action is needed.
A friendly city simply receives the army. Cities remain yours after your armies leave.

5. ATTACK
If an enemy army occupies the connected city, choose Attack city, then roll.
Attack total = the selected attacking army's strength + one six-sided die (1–6).
Defence total = all defending armies' combined strength in that city + one six-sided die (1–6).
Higher total wins. A tie leaves city control unchanged. An attack costs 1 action even if it fails or ties.

6. BATTLE RESULTS — HANDLED AUTOMATICALLY
Attacker wins: capture the city and move in. The attacking army loses 1 strength, keeping at least 1; defenders lose 2 in total and retreat.
Defender wins: the attacker stays in its original city and loses 2 strength. Defenders lose 1 in total, keeping at least 1 overall.
Tie: each force loses 1 strength in total, and the city keeps its owner. Strength never falls below zero.
Defending losses come from the strongest army first.
Retreat goes automatically to the nearest friendly city and costs no action. If none remains, that army has zero strength.

7. REINFORCE
Spend 1 action and 1 reinforcement token to add up to 2 strength to an army at its own side's city.
Maximum: 10 strength per army. Tokens do not refill each year.
An army with zero strength cannot move or attack, but can return by reinforcing at a friendly city.

8. IF YOUR TEAM CANNOT ACT
First try another army or a reinforcement.
Pass action appears only when the ENTIRE team has no legal move, attack or reinforcement.
Each pass uses 1 action. Next team still requires zero actions remaining.

9. WINNING
Normal victory: after Qing finishes its two actions in 1864, click Show final score. The team controlling more cities wins.
Every city is worth 1 point. Equal city counts mean a draw.
Special Taiping victory: capture Beijing 北京 at any time to win immediately, regardless of the selected route filter.
Qing capturing Tianjing 天京 counts as one city; it does not immediately end the game.

10. READING THE MAP
City circles and names show current ownership. Soldier portraits show army locations; their numbers show strength.
Kaifeng 开封 and Huaiqing 怀庆 are different cities.
The northern route is Tianjing 天京 → Hefei 合肥 → Kaifeng 开封 → Huaiqing 怀庆 → Linqing 临清 → Cangzhou 沧州 → Tianjin 天津 → Beijing 北京.
Tianjing 天京 (Nanjing) is different from Tianjin 天津.

11. HISTORY CARDS
History cards provide classroom context. They do not change resources, deduct strength, block turns or require Accept/Decline choices.
There is no treasury, annual income, fortification or separate Claim action in this version.

12. CLASSROOM SETUP
Use one shared game on the classroom screen, with one operator entering the teams' decisions. Separate browser tabs or computers run independent games.
Sound starts on and can be muted. Audio does not affect the rules.
Refreshing or closing the page clears the current game. Use Settings → Save / restore text to keep a copy first.
Undo is available to correct input mistakes; repeating the same battle after undo gives the same dice.

30-SECOND INTRODUCTION
“Each year, Taiping takes two actions, then Qing takes two. Move, attack or reinforce. Moving into an undefended city captures it automatically. Battles use strength plus a die. After 12 years, the team with more cities wins—but Taiping wins immediately if it captures Beijing.”
